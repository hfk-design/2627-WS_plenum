A warm welcome to the supervision of your BA / MA projects (BA thesis / MA thesis / individual master supervision).

# Requirements

- **Required** – Kick-off, interim presentation and final presentation.
- **Recommended** – We meet every other week in BA / MA groups. Come with questions, progress, work-in-progress, etc. It’s not required to always join, but it’s recommended, as it’s an opportunity for feedback and a structure. Especially if you’re working on your thesis, it’s helpful to share your process along the way and leave room for iterations – instead of showing up at the end with something finished, when there might not be enough time left for feedback.
- **Optional** – Feel free to join any week (even if it’s the other group’s week) if you want feedback on something specific or if you want the structure. You can also form peer groups and use Wednesdays as a co-working day.

### Please note

- We’re a big group this semester – so the more regularly you come, the more feedback and support I can give you along the way.
- If you miss a session (e.g. the kick-off), make sure to get all relevant info from your fellow students – so I don’t have to repeat things we’ve already discussed.
- Please be on time – **we start 10:00 sharp**.
- If you don’t want to participate in the plenum, let me know and I’ll remove you from the list / Teams.
